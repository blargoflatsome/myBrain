# My Brain

An app for storing everything you need to remember, fast to capture into and calm to use. These are its own words; the rules live in [docs/SPEC.md](docs/SPEC.md).

## Capturing

**Orphan**:
A captured thought that hasn't been filed as a task, Vault item, quest, habit or Post It yet.
_Avoid_: Inbox, capture queue

## Tasks and priority

**High task**:
A task at the High priority level. Saying "priority task" means the same thing.
_Avoid_: Priority tab

**Priority habit**:
A habit marked Priority, such as taking medication.

**Priority reminder**:
The full-screen reminder for a High task or priority habit, which stays up until it's completed, snoozed or cancelled.

## The Vault and quests

**Bucket**:
A named list in The Vault that holds items to remember.
_Avoid_: List, time bucket

**Time needed**:
How long a quest takes: 30 min, 2 hours, Half a day or A full day.
_Avoid_: Time bucket

**Energy needed**:
How much energy a quest takes: Ugh, Fun, Needs setup or Medicated.

## Trophies

**Trophy**:
A reward for something done in the app, most with levels from Bronze to Legendary.
_Avoid_: Achievement

**First**:
Any trophy for doing something for the first time.
_Avoid_: Badge

## People

**Connection**:
Someone on My Brain where both people agreed to connect.
_Avoid_: Friend

**Offsite connection**:
Someone saved by email or phone to invite to events, who needs no account.
_Avoid_: My people, Your people, saved people

**Contacts**:
The window that lists connections and Offsite connections, for inviting people to an event.

**Invitee**:
Anyone invited to an event, connection or guest.
_Avoid_: Attendee (except as the event form's Attendees fold-out)

**Guest**:
An invitee who answers through a private link instead of in the app, with or without an account.

**Answer**:
What an invitee says to an invitation: pending (not yet), Yes, Maybe, No, or Dropped out. Invitees who said No or dropped out no longer count when the event changes or is cancelled, and the event leaves their side of the app.
_Avoid_: RSVP

## Alerts

**Alert**:
One item under the bell, kept for 7 days.
_Avoid_: Today's notifications

**Push**:
A notification sent to a device where Reminders are turned on.

**Reminder**:
An alert at a time the user set: for a habit, routine, High task or event, or the evening check-in. Usually pushed too.
